WHAT ARE META GLASSES?

Meta Glasses are AI-powered smart glasses developed by Meta and available at FramesDirect.com. They feature built-in Meta AI, open-ear speakers, a discreet camera, and are compatible with prescription lenses — combining everyday eyewear with hands-free technology.

CAN I GET META GLASSES WITH MY PRESCRIPTION?

Yes. Meta Glasses are available with prescription lenses compatible with a total power range of -8.00 to +6.00, covering approximately 95% of prescription wearers. They are available as Rx only at FramesDirect.com.

HOW MUCH DO META GLASSES COST?

Meta Glasses start at $299 for Clear and Sun Plano lenses. Polar Sun lenses are available from $329, and Transitions® lenses from $379. The Kylie Jenner Creator Collection starts at $399.

WHAT FRAME STYLES ARE AVAILABLE FOR META GLASSES?

At launch, Meta Glasses are available in three styles: the Adventurer (Rectangle, available in Standard and Large sizes), the Fury (Square), and the Starfire (Oval, part of the Creator Collection). Additional styles — including Cat Eye (Capri) and Pantos (Nova) — will launch in Fall 2026.

WHAT LENS OPTIONS ARE AVAILABLE?

Meta Glasses come in four lens types: Clear Plano, Sun Plano, Polar Sun, and Transitions® GenS — all available with prescription. A range of lens colors is available depending on the frame and style chosen.

WHAT IS INCLUDED IN THE BOX WITH META GLASSES?

Every pair of Meta Glasses includes a matching charging case that provides up to 40 hours of total battery life (glasses + case combined). The case is available in colors coordinated to match the frame.

ARE META GLASSES COMPATIBLE WITH META AI?

Yes. Meta Glasses are powered by Meta AI, accessible hands-free via the assignable action button on the temple. Features include voice control, real-time translation, messaging, and more through the Meta AI app.

HOW DO I CHARGE META GLASSES?

Meta Glasses charge via the included charging case. For extended use, the Boracay Meta Glasses Charging Stand (sold separately, $59) provides a full charge in 75 minutes and is compatible with Meta Glasses at FramesDirect.com.

HOW DO I RETURN META GLASSES?

Meta Glasses follow the standard FramesDirect.com return policy for electronic eyewear. If you believe your glasses are defective after the return window has closed, you can open a warranty claim at meta.com/help/support.
