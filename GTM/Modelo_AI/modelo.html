FAQ

WHAT ARE META JOINT GLASSES?

Meta Joint Glasses are AI-powered smart glasses developed by Meta and available at FramesDirect.com. They feature built-in Meta AI, open-ear speakers, a discreet camera, and are compatible with prescription lenses — combining everyday eyewear with hands-free technology.

WHAT ARE META JOINT GLASSES?

Yes. Meta Joint Glasses are available with prescription lenses compatible with a total power range of -8.00 to +6.00, covering approximately 95% of prescription wearers. They are available as Rx only at FramesDirect.com

HOW MUCH DO META JOINT GLASSES COST?

Meta Joint Glasses start at $299 for Clear and Sun Plano lenses. Polar Sun lenses are available from $329, and Transitions® lenses from $379. The Kylie Jenner Creator Collection starts at $399.

WHAT FRAME STYLES ARE AVAILABLE FOR META JOINT GLASSES?

At launch, Meta Joint Glasses are available in three styles: the Adventurer (Rectangle, available in Standard and Large sizes), the Fury (Square), and the Starfire (Oval, part of the Creator Collection). Additional styles — including Cateye (Capri) and Pantos (Nova) — will launch in Fall 2026.

WHAT LENS OPTIONS ARE AVAILABLE?

Meta Joint Glasses come in four lens types: Clear Plano, Sun Plano, Polar Sun, and Transitions® GenS — all available with prescription. A range of lens colors is available depending on the frame and style chosen.

WHAT IS INCLUDED IN THE BOX WITH META JOINT GLASSES?

Every pair of Meta Joint Glasses includes a matching charging case that provides up to 40 hours of total battery life (glasses + case combined). The case is available in colors coordinated to match the frame.

ARE META JOINT GLASSES COMPATIBLE WITH META AI?

Yes. Meta Joint Glasses are powered by Meta AI, accessible hands-free via the assignable action button on the temple. Features include voice control, real-time translation, messaging, and more through the Meta AI app.

HOW DO I CHARGE META JOINT GLASSES?

Meta Joint Glasses charge via the included charging case. For extended use, the Boracay Meta Joint Glasses Charging Stand (sold separately, $59) provides a full charge in 75 minutes and is compatible with Meta Joint Glasses at FramesDirect.com.
